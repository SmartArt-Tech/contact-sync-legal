Privacy policy

Contact Sync (Android app) · Last updated: 5 October 2026

Contact Sync (“the app”) is published by SmartArt Tech (“we”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what control you have. In short: your contacts go between your phone and your Google account and nowhere else.

What the app accesses

  • Contacts on your phone, through the Android contacts permission you grant: names, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses, organisations and job titles, birthdays, notes, websites and nicknames.
  • Your Google Contacts, through the Google People API, after you sign in with Google. The app requests only the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts. It reads the same kinds of fields as above, plus your contact labels (groups), so you can choose which labels to copy. It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, your profile, or Google’s automatically saved “Other contacts”.
  • Optional CardDAV mode: if you choose to use a CardDAV server instead, the app uses the server address and the login (for example an app password) you enter.

How the information is used

Only to perform the synchronisation you ask for: copying new and changed contacts between your phone and your Google account, detecting duplicates and conflicts, and showing you the result. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

Where it is processed and stored

  • On your device. All processing happens inside the app on your phone. Contact data is sent directly between your phone and Google (or your chosen CardDAV server) over encrypted connections.
  • We run no servers for this app, so we never receive, store or can see your contacts, your Google sign-in, or your usage.
  • The app keeps, in its private storage on your phone: your sign-in token (encrypted with the Android Keystore where the device supports it), a table that links each phone contact to its Google contact, your settings, and a short log of recent sync activity that may contain contact names. None of this leaves your phone.
  • The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no crash-reporting service.

Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone. The only parties that receive contact data are Google (to store your contacts in your Google account, under Google’s own privacy policy) and, if you choose that mode, the CardDAV server you configure.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Contact Sync’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Signing in from a computer

If your phone has no web browser, the app can show a temporary page on your local network so you can sign in with a browser on your computer. The page is served by the phone itself, is only reachable at a secret address shown on the phone, stops after you finish (or after 10 minutes), and the one-time sign-in code is useless without a secret that never leaves the phone.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Sign out in the app’s Settings removes the stored sign-in token from the phone.
  • Revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything the app stored on the phone.
  • Contacts that were copied to your phone or to your Google account are your contacts; they stay there until you delete them in the Contacts app or in Google Contacts.

Children

The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, since we receive no data at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new “last updated” date.
