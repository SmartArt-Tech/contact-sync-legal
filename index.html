Contact Sync

Keeps your phone's contacts and your Google Contacts in step, on phones that have no touchscreen and no Play Store.

Contact Sync is a small Android app for simple phones that are operated with the navigation keys, such as rugged and flip-style models, where the usual sync tools are missing. Everything in the app works with the Up/Down/OK/Back keys and the number keys; no touchscreen or browser is needed on the phone.

What it does

  • Two-way sync between the contacts on the phone and your Google Contacts, or one-way in either direction if you prefer.
  • Duplicate-aware. Phone contacts are sent first, and anyone who already exists on both sides is matched and merged instead of copied twice.
  • Conflict handling. If the same contact changed on both sides, choose “ask me”, “phone wins” or “Google wins”. When asked, you see what differs and pick the phone version, Google’s, or keep both.
  • Automatic or manual. Sync every 15 minutes up to once a day, or only when you press the button; optionally on Wi-Fi only.
  • Choose what comes over. Pick which Google labels (groups) to copy to the phone. Google’s automatic “Other contacts” are never copied.
  • Safety nets. Deletions can be switched off, a sync that would remove most of your contacts is stopped, and a Stop button is always available.

How you sign in

You sign in with your Google account and approve a single permission: access to your contacts. If your phone has no web browser, the app shows a temporary page on your Wi-Fi so you can finish the sign-in in a browser on your computer. Your Google password never reaches the app, and you can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your data stays yours
  • Uses the Google People API with the permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/contacts, only to synchronise your contacts with your phone.
  • Does not read your email, files, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Contact data travels directly between your phone and Google. The developer runs no server and never receives your data.

Requirements

Android 6.0 or newer, a Google account, and a network connection for the sync itself.
